Content Element 2 - Park

Named after the sunsets that illuminate the city and its skies, Sunset Parks self titled park makes the true significance of the areas name clear, as its elevated position provides a full view of manhattan, as well as the statue of liberty and the surrounding burroughs. The park is a great place to meet with, and make; friends of all ages, wether at the basketball courts, or the pool that opens in the summer.



Content Element 3 - Music

Anywhere you go in Sunset Park, you will most likely to encounter a vibrant music scene that reflects the area's diverse cultural heritage from cars, live preformaces, doorsteps, and a great amount of barbershops.
